## Download and play

| You have | Download | Then |
|---|---|---|
| **Windows 10/11** | `StrongholdAssault-…-Windows-Setup.exe` | Double-click it, click **Next** → **Install** → **Finish**. It starts the game and puts it on your Desktop and Start Menu. No admin rights needed. |
| Windows, no install | `StrongholdAssault-…-Windows-Portable.zip` | Unzip anywhere, double-click `StrongholdAssault.exe`. |
| **Linux** (64-bit) | `StrongholdAssault-…-Linux.tar.gz` | Unpack, then run `./install.sh` (adds it to your apps menu), or just run `./StrongholdAssault.x86_64`. |

**Windows SmartScreen** may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't code-signed: click **More info** → **Run anyway**.

Pick a side on the menu; the computer plays the other one. `Esc` pauses. If it runs slowly, set **Lighting** to **Standard** on the menu. See *How to play* in the Start Menu (or `HOW_TO_PLAY.txt`) for the keys.

Needs a 64-bit PC with a Vulkan-capable graphics card (older cards fall back to OpenGL) and about 1 GB of disk.
