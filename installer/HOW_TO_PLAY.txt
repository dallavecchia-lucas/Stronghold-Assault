STRONGHOLD ASSAULT - QUICK START
================================

Pick a side on the main menu. The computer plays the other side.

  ATTACKER (real-time strategy) wins by destroying every air vent on the
  stronghold's roofs. You get one war chest (25,000 credits) and no income:
  every unit lost is gone for good.

  DEFENDER (first person) wins by holding the stronghold for 25 minutes.
  You start with nothing and earn credits by killing attackers.

Esc pauses the game (both sides).
Too slow? On the main menu set Lighting to "Standard" instead of "Ray-traced".


DEFENDER KEYS
-------------
  1-9        take over a post (machine guns, cannons, riflemen)
  G          the Gunner Squad          Tab   jump to the busiest post
  WASD       move      Shift sprint    Space jump
  Left mouse fire      Right mouse aim/zoom     R reload
  B / N / M  buy an AA Squad / Flame Squad / Infiltrator Squad
  K / J / H  buy a Sniper / Fighter Jet / Hover Tank
  F1-F6      take command of something you bought
  C          call a drone swarm to your post


ATTACKER KEYS
-------------
  Camera     WASD / arrows / screen edge to pan, Q/E to rotate, wheel to zoom
  Left mouse click or drag to select (Shift adds)
  Right mouse attack-move, or attack what you click
  Ctrl+Right mouse   move without stopping
  Backspace  select your base, then a unit's key to build it:
             Z Bear Rifleman   C Wolf Medic     N Mule Artillery
             V Sky Talon       B War Elephant   I Combat Rhino
             L Bison Field Gun K Hippo Gunner   M Breaching Ram
             J Night Ops Chimp X Kangaroo       H Giraffe Scout
             Y Armored Lioness U Serpent Sniper
  Ctrl+1-9   save a group, 1-9 to recall it
  F1         show all keys in game

Only Breaching Rams, Chimps and Kangaroo Commandos can get inside once a
gate is down.

SYSTEM NEEDS
------------
  64-bit Windows 10/11 or Linux, a graphics card with Vulkan support
  (most cards from 2016 on; older ones fall back to OpenGL), about 1 GB of disk.
